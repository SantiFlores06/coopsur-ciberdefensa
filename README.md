# CoopSur Ciberdefensa

**Security and risk analysis case studies — one real incident, one fictional organization.**

![status](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-in%20progress-yellow)

This repository is part of my preparation for a career in cybersecurity, with a long-term goal of
security leadership. Each case is analyzed the way a security team would: what was at stake, how it
could fail (or did fail), and what should change.

## Cases

| # | Case | Type | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Binance hack | Real incident | 🟡 In progress |
| 2 | CoopSur — a fictional cooperative | Fictional organization | 🟡 In progress |

## How each case is structured

<!-- Adjust to the method you actually use. -->

1. **Context** — the organization and what it does
2. **Assets** — what needs protecting
3. **Threats and vulnerabilities** — who could attack, and how
4. **Risk assessment** — likelihood × impact
5. **Controls and recommendations** — what to change, prioritized

> Write-ups are in Spanish.
